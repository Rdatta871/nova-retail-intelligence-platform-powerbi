# Nova Retail Intelligence Platform

## Overview

Nova Retail Intelligence Platform is an end-to-end Power BI analytics solution built to help retail businesses transform data into actionable business intelligence.

The dashboard brings together Sales, Products, Customers, Employees, and Customer Feedback into a single reporting platform, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## Project Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance
- Track revenue and order trends
- Analyze product and category performance
- Understand customer demographics and loyalty behavior
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and service quality
- Explore AI-powered business insights

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of business performance through key KPIs and revenue trends.

### Sales & Product Intelligence
Analyzes revenue drivers, top-performing products, and sub-category performance.

### Customer Intelligence
Explores customer demographics, loyalty tiers, and regional distribution.

### Customer Experience
Monitors customer feedback, issue types, customer satisfaction, and support performance.

### AI Insights Hub
Showcases advanced visual analytics including decomposition trees, scatter analysis, KPI gauges, and ranking insights.
